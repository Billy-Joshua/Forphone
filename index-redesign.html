Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying and selling with EstoreRW

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery within Kigali takes 24-48 hours. Other provinces take 3-5 business days. Express delivery available for 2-3 hours within Kigali for urgent orders.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Bank of Kigali transfers, PayPal, Cash on Delivery, and Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin). All payments are 100% secure and encrypted.

Do you offer warranty?

All phones come with a 30-day money-back guarantee. Extended warranties from 6 months to 2 years are available for an additional fee. All units are verified authentic.

How do I sell my phone?

Fill out the "Sell Your Phone" form with your phone's details. We'll give you an instant quote. If you accept, we handle free pickup and payment within 48 hours via your preferred payment method.

Are the phones authentic?

100% authentic and original. Every phone is verified and tested before shipment. We have partnerships with official distributors and provide original packaging and warranties.

What's your return policy?

Easy 30-day return policy. If the phone doesn't meet your expectations, return it in original condition for a full refund. No questions asked. We also cover return shipping.
